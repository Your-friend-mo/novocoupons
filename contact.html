Frequently Asked Support Questions

How can I submit a new coupon code?

You can send coupon details and merchant confirmation directly to editor@novocoupons.com. Our editors will verify and publish valid submissions within 24 hours.

What is the response time for customer support inquiries?

All support requests are reviewed within 2 hours during normal operating hours, and within 12 hours on weekends.

How do merchants update or dispute active promotions?

If you encounter a merchant who modified terms or expired a code ahead of schedule, please forward the retailer name and code details to our team so our editors can review and update the listing immediately.
